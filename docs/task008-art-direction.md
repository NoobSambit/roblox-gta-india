# Task 008 — Victoria interior art direction

Task 007 is the fixed gameplay foundation: seven rooms, 18 loot markers, nine
security reservations, four bidirectional exits and two restricted volumes.
No Studio geometry, marker transform, approved exterior or shell is changed.

## Art language
Warm ivory and mushroom-grey classical surfaces, charcoal exhibit furniture,
muted brass edges and deep teal public wayfinding. Restricted rooms add restrained
oxblood thresholds and small amber equipment lenses. Plain material colours plus
four flat painting textures; no PBR maps, ornate carvings or bevel-heavy detail. Existing columns,
arches and dome remain the architectural focus. New architecture is shallow,
modular enhancement for the existing partitions and display zones.

Six coordinated ImageGen room references establish palette, proportion, display
and device language. They are mood/design guides, never dimensioned floor plans.
Task 007 coordinates and footprints always override generated image details.
Four additional original ImageGen artworks are flat, unframed 2D images, released
at 256–512 px with their aspect ratio preserved. Only prominent future pieces
may justify up to 1024 px. Reuse their image IDs; never model painting artwork
as 3D geometry. Physical frames/mounts remain inside the normal geometry budget.

## Reusable scope
Architecture: simple column/pilaster, ten-stud-clear arch and restricted portal
trims, short dado/cornice modules, square/octagonal plinths, low barrier and blank
feature panel. Use new columns only at reserved walls; do not duplicate or replace
existing hall columns.
Plain wall banners and thin floor runners provide broad colour accents without
covering windows or adding exhibit clutter.

Exhibition: small/medium/premium cases, landscape/portrait frames, document desk,
secure archive cabinet/shelf, information stand and plaque. Case glazing is a
separate simple mesh/material group. Protected-case enhancements belong inside
the premium case budget.

Loot: small folio/letter, flat original river/street/garden/city artwork images,
decorative miniature, trade/presentation sets, abstract bronze, ceremonial vessel,
keepsake, angular insignia and rare archive folio. These are fictional objects,
not representations claimed to exist in the real museum.

Security: static emitter, separately pivoted moving-laser housing, flush electrical
floor module and terminal. No beams, scripts or functioning indicators.

## Geometry and placement
One coordinate unit equals one Roblox stud; author Y-up, Blender (x,-z,y).
Assets export centred at their ground/mount origin with unit transforms.
Ordinary exhibit supports stay at or below the original 4 × 4 footprint;
protected cases stay within the reserved 6 × 6 footprint. Keep ten-stud partition
openings, nine-stud courtyard doors and established routes intact.

Record per-asset triangle counts and budget caps, material-separated mesh count,
dimensions, pivot, mounting offsets and collision recommendation. Report unique
library triangles separately from the proposed repeated heist instance total.
Aim for a lean library and roughly 10k–22k additive triangles in the mapped pass;
do not add detail merely to spend a budget.

The placement manifest binds every existing loot/security ID to asset choices
without relocating the source markers. Queen's Hall receives only sparse
wayfinding; optional architecture dressing is explicitly distinguished from the
mapped pass. The upper volume remains untouched.

## Production and delivery
1. Inspect Task 007, lock its source hashes/coordinates, save this direction.
2. Generate and select exactly six coordinated room concepts; save full prompts.
3. Build a categorized Blender library, reusable material-separated geometry and
   asset-sized editable sources.
4. Export individual Roblox FBXs with project-standard Stud/1 import guidance.
5. Render original per-asset PNGs, category sheets and a few assembly vignettes.
6. Perform only mesh/normals/pivot/scale/triangle/export sanity checks. No Studio
   import, gameplay, multiplayer or device-performance claims.
7. Save and push exactly three detailed commits: concept plan/references; editable
   library/sources/exports; assembled reviews/validation and integration handoff.

Generated references may simplify or suggest presentation; no reference image
authorizes changed circulation. No third-party models, fonts, textures or music
will be adopted. Original flat ImageGen illustrations supply painting content;
all other material zones remain plain colours with no PBR texture dependency.

# Task 001 — final environmental design

Create the actual game's first environmental slice directly in Hood Testing (96105551033653), in one continuous place. The user's brief and subsequent direction authorise autonomous construction. No gameplay systems, movable vehicles, Victoria Memorial or Howrah Bridge are included.

The main road is commercial. Its combined plots contain three original heritage anchors: an offset-wing emporium with an octagonal corner pavilion and faceted copper cupola; a U-shaped exchange with a walk-in court and entrance bridge; and a multi-gabled market with a covered through passage. Five simpler commercial blocks complete that frontage. Shape, depth, roofline and usable voids take priority over intricate ornament.

Sixteen residential buildings occupy the back lanes and expanded outer loop. Four reusable British-era North Kolkata-inspired townhouse types (veranda, arcade, red gallery and corner) form Generic 1.0, mixed with simple houses and apartments. Three buildings have ground-floor shop alcoves. Kits support colour variants.

Original static Kolkata-inspired dressing includes the chai and adda courtyard, blue-and-white railings, stoops, shutters, awnings, produce counters, small shop displays, benches, forecourts, planters, lamps, utilities and low boundary walls. Keep crossing and lane openings clear. Finish construction and visual dressing before final performance testing, as requested by the user.

Use a warm City of Joy afternoon palette: cream, dusty coral, ochre, sage, deep green, copper and restrained civic blue. Preserve readable shadows, warm practical lights, muted haze and light grading. Avoid expensive overlapping transparency, particle fields, shadowed point lights and runtime generation.

Structure: Workspace/World/Cities/Kolkata/Districts/Para01; reusable kits and edit-only modules in ServerStorage/WorldAuthoring. Studio owns the scene. Local sources preserve authoring logic without automatic sync. Preserve the original template and remove intermediate generated revisions.

Scale: main road 40, pavement 12, cross street 20, gullies 14–16, residential storeys 12–14 studs. Ground foundation 900 × 900; expanded lane loop approximately 540 × 500. Connections remain open for later Kolkata development and eventual continuous-world expansion.

After construction, inspect street/gully/courtyard/overview views; walk named routes in a real Studio client; verify respawn, clearances, anchoring, metadata and streaming configuration; record view-dependent rendering counters and screen limitations; save an engine-serialized recovery package. Physical mobile and multiplayer performance remain unverified unless actually tested.

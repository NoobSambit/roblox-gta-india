# Task 005 asset brief

Original stylised interpretations for the existing Indian Hood art direction.
No map assembly, water body, station, additional ghats or gameplay code.

- Howrah: 896-stud length, 60-stud road, two 10-stud pedestrian strips;
  deck Y=32, twin truss peaks Y=144, future water Y=0. Twenty-eight
  32-stud bays grouped into seven manageable 128-stud sections. Twin
  cantilever peaks, suspended centre, laced uprights, portal frames and
  upper lateral bracing follow the examined side/roadway photos.
- One upgraded public Kolkata Ganga ghat: Ahiritola/Bagbazar bathing access and
  Baje Kadamtala's broad terraces, three open blue-white barrel canopies,
  two small side shade bays, generous steps, landings and retaining cheeks.
  Approximately 149 x 128 studs. Promenade/street Y=13.5, water Y=0.
  No upper storey, windows or rooms. Street apron underside Y=12.7 embeds into
  the future road bank; river foundation and shallow street plinth are separate.
  Road entrance: broad horizontal blue-white entablature over three open
  archways, four substantial piers and buried footings; no isolated stick gateway.
- No exposed bridge piers in the river opening. Small bearing shoes remain at
  the bank/approach ends. User feedback superseded the first building-like ghat.
- Seven connection/support modules, fifteen prop designs and two small
  background service shells. No district generation or decorative scatter.
- Working assembled budgets: bridge 35–55k, ghat 8–15k, supporting modules
  5–12k, unique props 4–8k triangles. Individual meshes below 10k where possible.
- Shared opaque colour materials; no photographic textures. Linked Blender
  mesh modules, stable MeshKeys, and assembly manifests preserve reusable parts.
- Established neutral-unit / FBX Unit Scale export convention. Ground/water
  origins and approach sockets are recorded in the manifest. Geometry is static.
- One integrity/export inventory and brief visual review; fix observed issues.
  Studio collisions, Terrain Water and actual performance are later integration work.
